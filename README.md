## 📜 My Story

42 Kuala Lumpur is a coding school built around a project based and peer-to-peer learning enviroment.
Rather than relying primarily on traditional lectures and examinations, students learn by working on 
hands-on projects, solving problems independently, researching unfamiliar concepts, and collaborating 
with their peers to find solutions.

I joined 42 Kuala Lumpur after completing my SPM in 2024 because I wanted to learn the fundamentals of
programming. I was looking for an environment that would challenge me to think critically, solve problems 
independently, and understand how software works beyond simply writing code.

The peer-to-peer nature of 42 was also something that attracted me to the programme. Students are encouraged
to discuss problems, share knowledge, review each other's work, and learn from different approaches to the
same problem. This taught me that becoming a better programmer is not only about knowing how to write code,
but also about knowing how to understand a problem, ask the right questions, research effectively, and
communicate solutions with others.

Completing the Core Programme gave me a much deeper understanding of computer science and, more importantly,
changed the way I approach technical problems. Instead of simply looking for an answer, I learned to break
complex problems down, investigate how things work, and build solutions from first principles.
